# 🤝 Contributing to Bug-Free Travel Guides

Thank you for helping build the world's most trusted travel resource for bug-phobic, arachnophobic, and insect-averse adventurers! 

Whether you have discovered a surprisingly bug-free hideaway, want to refine seasonal dates, or want to warn fellow travelers about a seasonal bug boom, your input is welcome.

---

## 📌 Ways You Can Contribute

1. **Submit a New Destination Guide:** Add a markdown file in the `destinations/` folder.
2. **Improve an Existing Guide:** Clarify seasonal bug emergence dates, add recommended hotels, or flag local microclimates.
3. **Add Travel Tips:** Contribute to `tips/bug-phobic-traveler-handbook.md` with gear recommendations, hotel inspection checklists, or coping strategies.
4. **Report Bug Alerts / Updates:** Open an [Issue](../../issues) if a previously safe destination is experiencing unusual insect activity (e.g. unseasonal rain causing mosquito blooms).

---

## 📝 Destination Guide Template

When adding a new destination under `destinations/<destination-slug>.md`, please follow this structure:

```markdown
# 📍 [Destination Name, Country]

## Overview & Bug Safety Rating
- **Bug Safety Rating:** [1-5 🛡️]
- **Primary Pests Absent:** [e.g. Mosquitoes, biting flies, venomous spiders, cockroaches]
- **Potential Pests Present:** [Be transparent — e.g. non-biting midges near lakes]
- **Key Landscape / Climate Feature:** [e.g. Hyper-arid desert, subpolar oceanic, alpine winter]

---

## 🗓️ Seasonal Timeline & Best Times to Visit
- 🟢 **Optimal (Safest / 100% Bug-Free):** [Months] — Why it's safe.
- 🟡 **Moderate Caution:** [Months] — What to expect.
- 🔴 **Strict Avoidance / Bug Hatch Season:** [Months] — Why bug-phobic travelers should avoid this window.

---

## 🔬 Entomological & Environmental Facts
[Explain *why* this destination has few/no bugs: altitude, freeze-thaw cycles, absence of water, wind speed, etc.]

---

## 🏨 Where to Stay: Bug-Safe Accommodation Tips
- [Specific hotel / neighborhood / building advice]
- [Floor recommendations, sealed A/C tips, screen advice]

---

## 🧭 Bug-Safe Activities & Highlights
- [Things to do with peace of mind]

---

## ⚠️ Traveler Warnings & Caveats
- [Any specific areas, wet months, or microclimates to avoid]
```

## ⚖️ Criteria for "Bug-Free" Designation

To ensure our guides remain trustworthy for travelers with severe phobias, destinations must meet at least one of the following criteria during the recommended season:
1. **Zero native biting insects (mosquitoes, blackflies, horseflies).**
2. **Temperatures consistently at or below freezing (< 0°C / 32°F) or night frost preventing insect flight.**
3. **Hyper-arid environments with relative humidity consistently < 25% and absence of standing water.**
4. **High alpine elevations (> 1,800m / 6,000ft) during shoulder or winter seasons.**
5. **Island climates with continuous maritime gale winds preventing insect swarms.**

## 🚀 How to Submit Changes

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Create a branch** for your update: `git checkout -b guide/new-destination-name`.
3. **Commit** your changes with a clear message: `git commit -m "Add guide for Zermatt, Switzerland"`.
4. **Push** your branch: `git push origin guide/new-destination-name`.
5. **Open a Pull Request** with a summary of your personal experience or research citations.

Thank you for helping others explore the world without fear! 🌍✨
